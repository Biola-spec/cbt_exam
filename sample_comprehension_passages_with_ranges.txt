# Sample Comprehension Passages with Question Ranges

## Passage 1 (Questions 1-5)
Title: The Industrial Revolution

The Industrial Revolution, which began in Britain in the late 18th century, was a period of major industrialization that transformed largely rural, agrarian societies into industrial and urban ones. This transformation had profound effects on socioeconomic and cultural conditions, starting in Great Britain and later spreading to Western Europe, North America, and eventually the rest of the world.

The First Industrial Revolution, which began around 1760 and lasted until about 1840, was characterized by the mechanization of textile production, the development of iron-making techniques, and the increased use of steam power. The Second Industrial Revolution, from about 1870 to 1914, was marked by steel production, mass production techniques, and the widespread use of electricity.

Key inventions of this period included the steam engine, which powered machinery and locomotives, the spinning jenny and power loom for textile production, and the telegraph for communication. These innovations led to the growth of factories, urbanization, and significant changes in labor practices.

The Industrial Revolution brought both benefits and challenges. While it led to increased production, improved transportation, and higher standards of living for some, it also resulted in poor working conditions, child labor, and environmental degradation. These issues eventually led to labor reforms and the development of labor unions.

## Passage 2 (Questions 11-13)
Title: Climate Change Overview

Climate change refers to long-term shifts in temperatures and weather patterns. These shifts may be natural, such as through variations in the solar cycle. However, since the 1800s, human activities have been the main driver of climate change, primarily due to burning fossil fuels like coal, oil and gas.

Burning fossil fuels generates greenhouse gas emissions that act like a blanket around the Earth, trapping the sun's heat and raising temperatures. This phenomenon is known as the greenhouse effect.

The effects of climate change include rising sea levels, intense droughts, water scarcity, severe fires, and extreme weather events. These changes threaten the balance of all life on Earth and contribute to food and water insecurity, increased poverty, displacement, and economic disruption.

Scientists have been studying climate change for over a century. Modern climate science, driven by better technology and more data, has strengthened the evidence that human activities are the primary cause of recent climate change.

## Sample Questions

1. In which country did the Industrial Revolution begin?
A. United States
B. France
C. Germany
D. Britain

ANSWER: D

2. What time period marked the First Industrial Revolution?
A. 1760-1840
B. 1870-1914
C. 1800-1850
D. 1900-1950

ANSWER: A

3. Which of the following was NOT mentioned as a key invention of the Industrial Revolution?
A. Steam engine
B. Spinning jenny
C. Telephone
D. Telegraph

ANSWER: C

4. What was one negative consequence of the Industrial Revolution mentioned in the passage?
A. Decreased production
B. Improved transportation
C. Child labor
D. Better working conditions

ANSWER: C

5. What eventually developed as a result of the challenges faced during the Industrial Revolution?
A. Factory systems
B. Labor unions
C. Agricultural improvements
D. Urban planning

ANSWER: B

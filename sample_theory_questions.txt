1. Explain photosynthesis.
MARKS: 10
MARKING SCHEME: Award 2 marks for mentioning sunlight, 2 marks for chlorophyll, 2 marks for carbon dioxide and water, 2 marks for glucose production, and 2 marks for oxygen release.

2. State three causes of soil erosion.
MARKS: 6
MARKING SCHEME: Award 2 marks for each correct cause that is briefly explained. Accept deforestation, overgrazing, heavy rainfall, poor farming practices, and construction activities.

3. Describe two differences between plant and animal cells.
MARKS: 8
MARKING SCHEME: Award up to 4 marks per valid difference. Accept cell wall, chloroplasts, large vacuole, regular shape, and food storage differences where correctly explained.

1. What is the result of the following matrix multiplication?
[2 1] [1 0]
[1 1] [0 1]

A. [2 1]
   [1 1]
   
B. [1 2]
   [1 1]
   
C. [3 1]
   [1 2]
   
D. [2 2]
   [2 1]

ANSWER: A

2. Which of the following represents a 2x2 identity matrix?

A. [1 0]
   [0 1]
   
B. [0 1]
   [1 0]
   
C. [1 1]
   [1 1]
   
D. [0 0]
   [0 0]

ANSWER: A

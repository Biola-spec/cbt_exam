1. What is the result of adding these two matrices?
[2 1]   [1 0]
[1 1] + [0 1]

A. [3 1]
   [1 2]
   
B. [2 1]
   [1 1]
   
C. [1 2]
   [1 1]
   
D. [3 2]
   [1 1]

ANSWER: A

2. Which matrix represents a reflection over the x-axis?

A. [1  0]
   [0 -1]
   
B. [-1 0]
   [ 0 1]
   
C. [0 1]
   [1 0]
   
D. [-1  0]
   [ 0 -1]

ANSWER: A

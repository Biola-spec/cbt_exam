1. What is the result of adding these two matrices?

A. [2 1]
   [1 1]
   
B. [1 2]
   [1 1]
   
C. [3 1]
   [1 2]
   
D. [2 2]
   [2 1]

ANSWER: A

2. Which matrix represents a 90-degree rotation?

A. [0 -1]
   [1  0]
   
B. [1  0]
   [0  1]
   
C. [-1 0]
   [ 0 -1]
   
D. [0  1]
   [-1 0]

ANSWER: A

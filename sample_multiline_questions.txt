1. What is the capital of France?

A. Paris is the capital city of France and it is located in the northern-central 
   part of the country along the Seine River.
B. Marseille is a major city in southern France, located in the Provence-Alpes-Côte 
   d'Azur region on the Mediterranean coast.
C. Lyon is a major city in eastern France, located in the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes 
   region along the Rhône and Saône rivers.
D. Toulouse is a major city in southern France, located in the Occitanie region 
   along the Garonne river.

ANSWER: A

2. Which of the following statements about photosynthesis is correct?

A. Photosynthesis is the process by which plants convert light energy into 
   chemical energy in the form of glucose using carbon dioxide and water, 
   with oxygen being released as a byproduct.
B. Photosynthesis occurs only at night when plants can rest and process 
   the nutrients they have absorbed during the day.
C. Photosynthesis is the process by which plants absorb oxygen from the 
   atmosphere and release carbon dioxide as a waste product.
D. Photosynthesis takes place in the roots of plants where specialized 
   cells convert minerals from the soil into usable energy.

ANSWER: A
